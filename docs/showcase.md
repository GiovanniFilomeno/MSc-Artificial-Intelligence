# Selected Work

This page is a guided tour through the most representative work in the repository. It is designed for readers who want to evaluate technical range without browsing every assignment folder.

## Systems And Algorithms

| Project | Location | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAT solver suite | [Fourth Semester/SAT/Project](<../Fourth Semester/SAT/Project>) | Implements multiple SAT-solving strategies in C++17, including recursive solving, watched literals, CDCL-style solving, and PicoSAT integration. This is one of the clearest systems-style projects in the archive. |
| Planning and reasoning encodings | [First Semester/Planning and Reasoning](<../First Semester/Planning and Reasoning>) | Encodes sliding-puzzle and N-Queens problems as logical reasoning problems, combining formal modelling with executable notebooks and reports. |
| NP-completeness report | [Third Semester/Computability and Complexity](<../Third Semester/Computability and Complexity>) | Presents a theoretical computer science report on the Battleship decision problem and Karp reductions. |

## Machine Learning And Deep Learning

| Project | Location | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Unsupervised ML sequence | [Second Semester/Machine Learning - Unsupervised Learning](<../Second Semester/Machine Learning - Unsupervised Learning>) | Explores estimation theory, maximum likelihood, PCA, kernel PCA, ICA, factor analysis, scaling, and projection methods. |
