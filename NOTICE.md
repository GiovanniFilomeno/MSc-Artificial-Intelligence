## Ownership and reuse

The repository contains material with several kinds of ownership:

- Work authored solely by the repository owner.
- Group-project work authored jointly with other students. Where known, the relevant project documentation describes the owner's contribution.
- Assignment prompts, notebook scaffolding, helper code, and other course-supplied context that remains embedded in student submissions.
- Public datasets, trained or serialized artifacts, and other third-party resources used for coursework.
- Third-party software retained with its upstream license.

No repository-wide software license is currently granted. Unless a file or subdirectory includes an explicit license, treat the material as available for portfolio review and academic reference only. The presence of third-party or course-provided material does not grant permission to copy, modify, or redistribute it.

Student-authored submissions may contain embedded excerpts of an assignment prompt or starter code. Rights in those portions remain with their respective authors. Verify the applicable course rules and obtain permission before redistributing course-provided material. Do not submit this work, or a derivative of it, as your own coursework.

Automated checks cover common identifiers and high-confidence credential formats, but they are not a legal or security guarantee. External links, binary files, archives, notebook outputs, group-project media, and commit metadata require separate review.
